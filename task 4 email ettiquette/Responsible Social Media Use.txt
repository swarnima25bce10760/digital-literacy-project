Responsible Social Media Use 
? Do’s
* ?? Think before you post—ask yourself, “Would I be okay if a teacher or employer saw this?”
* ?? Be respectful and kind in comments and messages, even if you disagree.
* ?? Keep your personal information private and use privacy settings wisely.
* ?? Double-check information before sharing so you don’t spread fake news.
* ?? Use social media in a positive way—learn new things, connect with people, and grow your profile.
? Don’ts
* ? Don’t overshare personal details like your location, phone number, or passwords.
* ? Don’t get into online fights or post things in anger—you might regret it later.
* ? Don’t post anything offensive, inappropriate, or harmful to others.
* ? Don’t blindly trust or forward every post you see.
* ? Don’t let social media take over your time or affect your studies and well-being.
